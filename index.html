About

I care about two things: making AI useful, and making it understandable.

I hold a Bachelor of Information Technology from Universitas Brawijaya and a Master of Artificial Intelligence from Universitas Gadjah Mada. Most recently I worked as an AI Engineer at INaAI, shipping object detection models for retail and CCTV, LLM-driven data insights, and optimization for scheduling, routing, and stock planning.

Before that, I spent years in the newsroom at Times Indonesia — first as a content writer, later building the data and automation tools behind the platform. That mix is still how I work: build the system, then explain it so people can actually use it.
